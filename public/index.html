How it works

From dusty to done,
in five steps.

  1. 01

    Power on & select

    Switch on the control unit and start a cleaning cycle from the panel.

  2. 02

    Feed the mat

    Lay the mat on the roller tray — the rollers guide it into the chamber.

  3. 03

    Deep brush scrub

    The rotating brush drum sweeps across the surface, lifting dust and grit.

  4. 04

    Belt‑driven motion

    The motor drives the drum through the pulley and belt for steady, even cleaning.

  5. 05

    Clean mat, ready

    Take out a refreshed mat — the dirt stays inside the enclosed cabinet.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I buy Smart MaT?

Reach out through the contact form below or by phone/email. We'll understand your requirement, arrange a demo, share a quote, and schedule delivery and setup once the order is confirmed.

Is Smart MaT available now?

The prototype is ready and testing is complete. We're now taking enquiries and early orders — contact us to reserve units or book a demonstration.

Who is it designed for?

Hospitals, schools and offices — anywhere with high foot traffic where entrance and floor mats need frequent, hygienic cleaning.

Can I see a live demo before buying?

Yes. We can demonstrate Smart MaT at your site or ours so you can see it clean your own mats.

Do you offer bulk or institutional pricing?

Yes. For multiple units or campus‑wide deployments we provide a custom quote, including options for customisation to your mat sizes.

What about installation and maintenance?

We handle setup and walk your staff through operation. Annual maintenance is included with the Smart MaT + Care plan and available as an add‑on for other plans.

Does Restechx take on other projects?

Absolutely. Beyond Smart MaT we offer product development, project consulting, and business consulting (marketing & sales) across IoT, embedded systems, automation and AI/ML.
